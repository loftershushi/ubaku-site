Stay

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘

A private villa with room to breathe.

One group a day in Naka-Ubakubetsu, Biei. A former farmhouse built in 1968 and passed down for over half a century, fully renovated and opened in January 2026. Up to 10 guests have the whole house on a site of about 1,800 m². About 18 minutes by car from Asahikawa Airport, about 10 minutes by car from Biei Station.

Our Approach

How we stand with this place

Moved by This Place.
Moving This Place Forward.

まちを想い、まちを動かす

Biei and Higashikawa sit at the foot of the Daisetsuzan mountains in central Hokkaido. We want them to become richer, more attractive places. We are both players, running our own businesses here, and producers, creating new projects with local people and those who want to try something here.

01

Build Our Own.

We open a villa and work the fields. With a real stake in this land, we build and grow our own businesses. That practice is also the base for what we build with others.

02

Build Together.

Through planning, design, and support for opening and running a business, we work right beside people trying something new. We combine what is already here with fresh perspectives to shape new businesses.

03

Connect.

More people who visit, try something new, and take part. More joy in living here, more choices for work. One visit becomes a lasting connection.

Team

From across the sea, from a southern island, from this town.
Producer

Mashu

A short study program at the Higashikawa Japanese Language School led to a move to Japan. Now works at a real estate development company in Tokyo while building independent projects in Biei and Higashikawa: creating stays, delivering vegetables, and sketching out new ways for the region to work.

Field Director

Yamato

A former elementary school teacher who made the leap from Okinawa to Hokkaido. Now works the fields with farmers in Biei, connecting soil and people and bringing more people into the life of this town.

Connector

Yushin

Born in Biei. After training at Eiheiji, a head temple of the Soto Zen school, now serves as a monk in Asahikawa. Connects the vegetables of Biei, and the people behind them, with the world beyond the town.
